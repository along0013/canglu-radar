Content excerpt:
MSC stated that due to evolving security situation in the Middle East and restrictions around Hormuz Strait / Bab el-Mandeb, it suspended bookings to Middle East region until further notice and instructed vessels to proceed to safe areas.
Maersk stated that due to military conflict escalation in the Middle East and safety deterioration, future trans-Suez sailings via Bab el-Mandeb would be suspended temporarily, with ME11 and MECL rerouted via Cape of Good Hope until further notice.
